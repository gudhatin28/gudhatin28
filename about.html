About

I like the part of analytics where the numbers stop matching.

Data rarely lies, but it almost never tells the whole story on its own. That gap between the number and the explanation is where I do my best work.

I’m a BI/Data Analyst with an M.S. in Information Systems from Virginia Commonwealth University, School of Business.

I work across SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI to clean and validate data, investigate inconsistencies, identify patterns, and turn analysis into reporting that non-technical stakeholders can use.

My project work spans public-sector asset data, mortgage delinquency risk, Power BI workforce analytics, research-system development, and healthcare security/compliance. That mix taught me to be precise with data, documentation, and sensitive information.

I’m targeting entry-level BI Analyst and Data Analyst roles where I can combine analytical discipline, curiosity, and clear business communication.
