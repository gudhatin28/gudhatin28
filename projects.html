02Data Quality · Integration · Governance

Utility & Asset Data Integration · Henrico County

Turning fragmented asset data problems into a structured integration plan.

Identified and documented 10 data-quality and integration challenges, including duplicate records, missing asset IDs, inconsistent schemas and coordinates, outdated GIS layers, and siloed systems.

Contributed to the proposed Unified Utility & Asset Data Integration Framework (UADIF), mapping the identified issues to standardized schemas, GlobalAssetIDs, geospatial normalization, ETL-based integration, and governance controls.

The work also included a phased implementation roadmap covering discovery, standardization, integration/governance, authoritative GIS layers, and analytics.

03SQL · Python · Risk Analytics

Mortgage Delinquency Risk Analytics · Freddie Mac

Risk segmentation across ~22.5K loan records.

Analyzed approximately 22,500 mortgage loan records, focusing on borrower and loan characteristics including credit score, LTV, DTI, and loan amount.

Evaluated four machine-learning models — Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and SVM — and compared 0.5%, 1.0%, and 1.5% risk-threshold scenarios.

The project estimated that excluding the highest-risk 1.5% of loans could represent approximately $2.06B in potential savings while supporting a 98% loan-purchase objective. This is a projected scenario result, not realized savings.
