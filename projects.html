03SQL · Python · Risk Analytics

Mortgage Delinquency Risk Analytics · Freddie Mac

Risk segmentation across ~22.5K loan records.

Analyzed approximately 22,500 mortgage loan records, evaluated Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and SVM models, and investigated credit score, LTV, and DTI risk patterns.

Compared 0.5%, 1.0%, and 1.5% risk-threshold scenarios. The project estimated the highest-risk 1.5% could represent ~$2.06B in potential savings while supporting a 98% purchase objective; this is a projected scenario, not realized savings.
