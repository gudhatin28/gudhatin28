## 02 — Utility & Asset Data Integration | Henrico County
### Data Quality · Integration · Governance

Identified and documented **10 core data-quality and integration problems** across fragmented utility and infrastructure asset data, including duplicate records, missing asset IDs, inconsistent schemas and coordinates, outdated GIS layers, and siloed systems. Contributed to the **Unified Utility & Asset Data Integration Framework (UADIF)** and mapped the identified problems to standardized schemas, unique asset identifiers, geospatial normalization, ETL-based integration, authoritative layers, and governance controls.

`Data Quality` `Data Integration` `Data Governance` `Data Reconciliation` `GIS / Asset Data`

## 03 — Mortgage Delinquency Risk Analytics | Freddie Mac
### SQL + Python · Risk Segmentation & Scenario Analysis

Analyzed approximately **22,500 mortgage loan records** and evaluated **4 machine-learning models**: Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and SVM. Investigated risk patterns involving **credit score, LTV, and DTI**, and compared **0.5%, 1.0%, and 1.5% risk-threshold scenarios**. The project estimated that excluding the highest-risk **1.5% of loans** could represent approximately **$2.06B in potential savings** while supporting a **98% loan-purchase objective**.

`SQL` `Python` `Risk Analytics` `Scenario Analysis` `Machine Learning`
