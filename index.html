About

The part I chase is what the numbers are really saying.

Data rarely lies, but it almost never tells the whole story on its own. I enjoy the work that starts when the numbers stop matching: cleaning the data, tracing the inconsistency, testing the explanation, and presenting the result in a way a stakeholder can actually use.

My project work spans cross-system data quality, mortgage delinquency risk, Power BI reporting, customer churn, and healthcare security/compliance. I care about being precise with data, documentation, and sensitive information.

I am targeting entry-level BI Analyst and Data Analyst opportunities where I can combine analytical discipline with strong business communication.
