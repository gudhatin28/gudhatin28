About

I like the part of analytics where the numbers stop matching.

Data rarely lies, but it almost never tells the whole story on its own. That gap between the number and the explanation is where I do my best work.

I work across SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI to clean data, investigate inconsistencies, validate findings, and turn analysis into decision-ready reporting.

My work spans public-sector asset data, mortgage delinquency risk, Power BI workforce analytics, research-system development, and healthcare security/compliance. That mix taught me to be precise with both data and documentation.
